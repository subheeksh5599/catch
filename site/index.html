A buyer waiting where liquidations land

When a dreamDEX position is liquidated, the engine sells it into the public order book first. Catch is a vault that rests orders at exactly those prices, and cancels each one in the same block the position behind it changes.

How a catch happens

Every number is re-derived by the contract. The keeper only proposes, and anyone can run it.

  1. 01

    Read

    Every open position on a market, its account health and its bankruptcy price, all pinned to one block.

  2. 02

    Map

    Each position's liquidation price, and the window the engine is allowed to close it in: liq − spread to liq

  3. 03

    Rest

    The vault places a post-only order inside the window where the most forced size sits. The contract refuses it if any number is off.

  4. 04

    Re-check

    A position behind the order changes. Somnia Reactivity calls the vault in that same block, and the order is cancelled if its window moved away.

  5. 05

    Catch

    The engine force-closes a position into the book and it fills the vault. The vault offers it back at or past its entry, never below.

What the contract refuses

No owner, no admin key, no sweep. These rules are the whole permission model, each with tests in the repository.

An order outside a window

Every account an order cites must still be closable at its price. Otherwise it reverts with the window it missed.

Too much in one place

One order stays under a share of the vault, one market under a larger share and under a slice of that market's own open interest.

A pushed price

No quote while the mark sits too far from the oracle index, or the index is stale. A resting order is pulled on its next re-check.

A fire sale

A caught position is offered back at or past its entry price. Anyone can improve the price; nobody can make it worse.

Buying in to sell out

New shares can't be redeemed or moved for an hour, so a deposit timed around a visible catch stays through the unwind.

Taking liquidity

Every order is post-only. The vault never crosses the book and has no way to liquidate anyone.

Not done yet

Said here so nobody has to find it.

  • Testnet only. dreamDEX perps are not on mainnet yet. Everything here runs on Somnia Hideki with test USDso.
  • A small vault. The vault holds what the team could move onto Hideki, so its orders are tens of dollars, near the venue minimum.
  • The backstop-bidder stage. After the book, the engine hands positions to registered bidders. No public way to register was found on the testnet engine, so Catch covers the book stage alone.
  • Loss is possible. A vault that buys liquidations in a fast market can lose. The caps and the entry rule limit that loss; they do not remove it.
